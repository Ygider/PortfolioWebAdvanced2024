Pokémon Team Maker

Welcome, young trainer. This is a tool for helping you generate a Pokémon team randomly. It rolls 6 Pokémon from different regions and you pick the ones you like. Repeat until your team of six is complete.

The goal is to find a new use for the Pokémon stored in your Pokémon Home. When was the last time you brought out your Pidgeot? Or your Nidoking? Challenge the Elite Four again with this team, or face your friends' randomly generated teams. I hope this tool gives a second life to some of your forgotten Pokémon.
